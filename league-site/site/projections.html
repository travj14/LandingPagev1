Win chances come from 100,000 simulations of each starting lineup, built on Sleeper's projections and how players with similar projections actually scored in 2022–2024. Later weeks in the playoff and last-place odds are coin flips. A player ruled Out, Doubtful, IR, suspended or on PUP keeps what he's scored and is projected 0 from there. Everyone on this page shares one refresh: automatic every 10 minutes on game days from the first kickoff until the day's games are final, and anyone can refresh after a 1-minute cooldown.
